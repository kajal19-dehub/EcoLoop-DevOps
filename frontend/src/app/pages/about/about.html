Created for cleaner communities and smarter waste recovery

Why EcoLoop exists

Our platform was created to connect residents, volunteers, and recycler partners with a shared mission: reduce landfill waste, improve recycling quality, and restore the health of local neighborhoods through better coordination.

Who created it

EcoLoop was developed by GreenCity Collective with support from local volunteers, environmental advocates, and recycling community leaders. Every feature was designed around the realities of urban waste collection and the need for transparency.

What matters most

Simple requests, reliable pickup scheduling, status tracking, and reuse-focused reporting help residents make informed decisions and turn everyday waste into measurable environmental progress.
